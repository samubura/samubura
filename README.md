## Hi, I'm Samuele 👋

PostDoc researcher at the **University of Bologna** (DISI), working on **Digital Twins**, **Multi-Agent Systems** and the **Web of Things**.

I build the abstractions and the tooling that let autonomous agents and digital twins understand each other — hypermedia-based Digital Twin ecosystems, BDI agent programming, and knowledge-level environments for pervasive cyber-physical systems.

🔭 **Currently** — Digital Twin ecosystems in the edge-cloud continuum, and the socio-demographic extension of the Urban Digital Twin of Bologna at the Alma Human AI Research Center.
🎓 **Ph.D.** (2025) — *Digital Twins Ecosystems for Intelligent Applications in Healthcare*, advised by Prof. Alessandro Ricci.
🏆 Best Paper Awards at **EMAS 2024** and **WESAAC 2025**.
📝 Guest Editor for the *Future Generation Computer Systems* special issue on Digital Twin Ecosystems, and on the organising committee of **DIGITA** @ IEEE PerCom.

### Things I work on

- **HWoDT** — a toolchain to build interoperable, hypermedia-based Digital Twin ecosystems *(SoftwareX, 2025)*
- **JaKtA** — BDI agent-oriented programming in pure Kotlin
- **Hypermedia MAS** — agents that discover and use their environment through the Web
